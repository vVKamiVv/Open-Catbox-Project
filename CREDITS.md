# Credits

## shin

The Open Catbox Project is built on **[shin](https://github.com/DCNick3/shin)**, the open-source re-implementation of
Entergram's visual-novel engine created by **DCNick3**. Its engine, data-format libraries (`shin-core`) and tools
(`sdu`) are the foundation everything here builds on; this project would not exist without them.

The code was imported from shin's `master` branch at commit `2405086` (December 2025), the result of three years of
work (October 2022 to December 2025). Contributors, as they appear in shin's history:

- Nikita Strygin
- ⭐️NINIKA⭐️
- Nickie S
- Neurochitin
- meew0
- xmoe

Thank you, all of you.

## The English translation

The port is made to play **[umineko-catbox-english](https://github.com/andOlga/umineko-catbox-english)**, the English
translation patch for *Umineko: The Symphony of Catbox and Dreams* by **andOlga** and its contributors, who are
credited in the [patch's own README](https://github.com/andOlga/umineko-catbox-english#credits). The patch is not
part of this repository; get it from its authors.

## The game

*Umineko no Naku Koro ni* is by **07th Expansion**; the Switch release this port runs is by **Entergram**.
